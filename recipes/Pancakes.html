Pancakes Recipe

Description

This is a dish of North American origin, consisting of flat, round batter cakes cooked on a griddle or frying pan,

Ingredients

  • Pancake batter
    • All-purpose flour
    • Milk
    • Egg
    • Baking powder
    • Sugar
    • Melted butter
  • Toppings
    • Maple syrup
    • Fresh berries
    • Butter

Steps

  1. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and sugar in a large bowl.
  2. Mix the milk, egg, and melted butter in a separate bowl, then pour into the dry ingredients until combined.
  3. Heat a lightly greased griddle or frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour scoops of batter onto the pan and cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  5. Stack the pancakes on a plate and top with butter, fresh berries, and maple syrup.
