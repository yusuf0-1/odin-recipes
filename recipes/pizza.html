Pizza Recipe

Description

This is a dish of Italian origin, consisting of a flat round base of dough baked with a topping of tomatoes and cheese,

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
    • flour
    • Water
    • Yeast
    • Salt
    • Olive oil
  • Pizza sauce
    • Crushed tomatoes
    • Garlic
    • Herbs
  • Mozzarella cheese
    • Shredded
  • PepperOni Slices

Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough by mixing the flour, water, yeast, salt, and olive oil, then allow it to rise.
  2. Roll and stretch the dough into a round shape on a floured surface.
  3. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the surface of the dough.
  4. Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese generously over the sauce.
  5. Arrange the pepperoni slices evenly on top of the cheese.
  6. Bake in a preheated hot oven until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
